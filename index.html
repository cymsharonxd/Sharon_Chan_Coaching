How the work actually goes

Approach
Grounded

We start with where you actually are

Not the version of your life you think you should present. Sessions start with an honest read of the present — what's working, what's costing you, what you've been avoiding saying out loud.

Directional

Clarity before action

Most people don't need more options — they need to know which direction is actually theirs. We narrow before we plan, so the steps you take are steps you'll keep taking.

Accountable

Built to hold you to it

Insight fades without follow-through. Between sessions you'll have concrete commitments, not vague intentions — and space to be honest about what actually happened.
